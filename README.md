# My Favorite Movie Series: Harry Potter

## About
**Harry Potter** is a *fantasy* film series based on the books by J.K. Rowling. It follows a young wizard and his friends at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry.

## Why I Like It
- Magical world and creatures
- Great friendships
- Exciting adventures

## Top 3 Characters
1. Harry Potter
2. Hermione Granger
3. Ron Weasley

## Hogwarts Houses
- Gryffindor
- Hufflepuff
- Ravenclaw
- Slytherin
